My story

“What if a photograph didn’t have to stay still?”

I’m a photography graduate from OCAD University, and this business started from a spark — not a business plan. I’ve spent my life learning how a single photograph can hold an entire life: the light in a face, the way someone laughed, the moment you’d give anything to relive.

MEMENTO is my answer to that feeling. I direct each animation myself — curating the movement the way a photographer composes a frame — so your memory doesn’t just move. It feels like it was always meant to.

— the maker of MEMENTO
