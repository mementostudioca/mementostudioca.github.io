Quick start

  1. Connect the frame to a computer with the USB-C cable.
  2. Copy your photos or videos into the frame — name them 01, 02, 03… and they play in that order.
  3. Eject, unplug, power it on — it plays on its own, in a loop.
  4. Recharge any time with any USB-C adapter.
