# MEMENTO — Project Brief (from ChatGPT session summary)

## Brand
- Name: MEMENTO
- Slogan: "bringing memories to life"
- Direction: minimal + warm + romantic + welcoming
- Avoid: overly sad, grief-focused, memorial-only, AI/electronics feel
- Should feel like: premium photography/keepsake studio

## Core business idea
- Customer provides one meaningful still photo (person, family, couple, child, pet)
- Locally built AI system turns it into a short, subtle, natural animated video
- No per-generation cost; goal = subtle movement, not drastic change
- Core emotional idea: "Turn a cherished photograph into a living memory."
- Brand language:
  - "A photograph captures a moment. What if that moment could move again?"
  - "Keep the photograph. Bring the moment to life."

## Physical product
- Simple dedicated video display, NOT a traditional smart digital photo frame
- Rechargeable, short-video playback, internal/preloaded video, USB upload,
  automatic playback, seamless looping
- Power on → video plays; power off → screen shuts off
- Explicitly NOT needed: Wi-Fi, touchscreen, apps, accounts, subscriptions, menus
- Source frames from China at low cost; test samples before buying inventory
- Should feel like a keepsake/object, not cheap electronics
- Strong positioning: "No Wi-Fi. No App. No Subscription. Just turn it on."

## Target customers
- Priority: pet owners (dogs/cats), family/parents/grandparents/children,
  couples/weddings/anniversaries, nostalgic/old photograph lovers
- Secondary: memorial/remembrance
- Buyer ≠ user → position strongly as a GIFT business
- Start by testing pets + family gifts; don't brand whole company around memorials

## Products & pricing (initial test ranges, not fixed)
- Digital-only animated photo: CAD $19–29 (low-risk entry)
- Hero: Living Memory Frame (custom animation + physical rechargeable frame): CAD $79–139
- Premium gift package (frame + premium packaging + personalized card): CAD $139–179

## Etsy strategy
- Sell the emotion, not the technology
- Avoid titles like "AI photo animation" / "AI digital photo frame"
- Better positioning: personalized living memory, moving portrait,
  living memory frame, personalized memory gift
- Show transformation visually: still photo → animation → physical frame
- Etsy video should demonstrate, not explain
- Listing categories: Pet Memory Frame, Family Memory Frame,
  Moving Portrait Gift, Personalized Living Memory
- Etsy = validation/sales channel first; independent website later

## Marketing
- Channels: Etsy, TikTok, Instagram Reels
- Hook: "What if your favorite photograph could move?"
- Recurring concept: "If this photograph could move…"
- Show emotional transformations, not AI talk
- Differentiator: photographer's eye curating/directing each animation

## Personal story / differentiation
- Photography graduate from OCAD University
- Passion for photography, visual storytelling, emotional power of photographs
- Idea came from "a spark", not a commercial concept
- Should feel like a real independent creative brand, not anonymous AI business
- Don't over-emphasize AI in the brand story

## Etsy profile structure
- "About MEMENTO": what MEMENTO is, philosophy/product, customer experience
  - "A photograph captures a moment. What if that moment could move again?"
  - Preserve original photo + subtle life; simplicity of the physical frame
- "My Story": who you are — OCAD grad, passion, visual storytelling, the spark
  - Core: "What if a photograph didn't have to stay still?"

## Draft shop profile elements
- Shop name: MEMENTO
- Tagline: bringing memories to life
- Shop title: Bringing Your Favorite Photos to Life
- Short bio: "Photography graduate from OCAD University creating personalized
  living memories from the photographs you love."
- Longer bio: "Photographer & creator of MEMENTO. OCAD University photography
  graduate with a passion for turning meaningful photographs into living memories."

## Logo direction (approved)
- Minimalist line art, warm cream/beige backgrounds, soft blush/pink accents,
  delicate black/charcoal typography, subtle romantic elements
- Motifs: heart, play triangle, photo/frame icon, elegant MEMENTO typography,
  "bringing memories to life" underneath
- Final brand: MEMENTO / bringing memories to life
- (Japanese-inspired direction also explored: calm, restrained, negative space,
  muted earthy colors, occasional red seal accent)

## Overall thesis
- NOT selling a digital photo frame — selling a physical way to experience a
  cherished photograph as a living memory. The frame is just the delivery mechanism.
- Strongest positioning: Photography + memory + simple technology
  (not AI + electronics)

## Recommended next steps
1. Prototype/test several Chinese frame models; pick one reliable minimalist frame
2. Create 10–20 excellent sample animations (pets, family, couples, nostalgic portraits)
3. Build Etsy around 2–3 strongest use cases first
4. Test which audience converts best
5. Price around emotional value, not hardware cost
6. Validate demand via Etsy + TikTok + Instagram before buying large inventory
